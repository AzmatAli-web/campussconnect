## Data and design dispositions

* **Identity and scope.** The [user model](../backend/src/models/user.js) and governance records hold profile and role information. University and department identifiers scope records and audiences. Institution and course catalog administration from the conceptual ER should only be shown as delivered where a real route exists.
* **Events and societies.** The [content schemas](../backend/src/models/campus-content.js) bind an event to its organizer, organizer type and optional society. Event registrations have a unique event/student pair. Society-manager assignments and [student membership](../backend/src/models/society-membership.js) are separate relationships.
* **Files and ownership.** [Resource metadata](../backend/src/models/academic-resource.js) and authenticated downloads enforce faculty ownership and publication state. Durable cloud file storage, retention policy and malware handling require independent pilot evidence.
* **Reports and notifications.** Listing reports belong to the [marketplace model](../backend/src/models/marketplace.js). Lost/found moderation and private complaint transitions follow their own models. Shared announcements and personal inbox records have different read/recipient relationships. Keep these distinctions in the ER and sequence diagrams.
* **Final FYP diagrams.** Show Flutter and one role-based web portal calling Express; Firebase provides authentication and optional push, and MongoDB stores shared application data. Show device-local planning separately. Label messaging and RAG as future components. The final signed diagram pack, role journeys and UI-state screenshots still require team review.
